### 👨‍💻 About Me

- 🎓 **B.Sc. in Computer Engineering** at the Federal University of Sergipe (**UFS**), Brazil.
- ⚡ **Technical Degree in Electrotechnics** from the Federal Institute of Sergipe (**IFS**).
- 💻 **Software Development & System Integration:** Hands-on experience building and sustaining web platforms, **REST APIs (FastAPI, NestJS)**, relational databases (**PostgreSQL, SQLite**), distributed messaging (**Java + RabbitMQ/AMQP**), and **Python/Linux CLI automation**.
- 🔧 **Firmware & Embedded Systems:** Bare-metal and RTOS engineering with **C, C++, FreeRTOS, ESP32/ESP32-S3, CH32V003 (RISC-V), Linux Kernel Modules**, and bench hardware debugging.
